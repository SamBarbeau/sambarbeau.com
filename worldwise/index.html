A game of perspective.

Country matchups are generated from real statistics. Both countries use the same measure and year, with a meaningful gap between them.

Each question shows the data year. “What counts?” explains the measure. Figures are rounded for readability.

Data: World Bank, World Development Indicators ↗ (CC BY 4.0).

Silhouettes: Natural Earth ↗, public domain. Some show the mainland or main islands for readability. Shapes are not drawn to a shared scale.
